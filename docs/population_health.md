# Population Health and Epidemiology

This package now includes tools for population-level health analytics:

- **Epidemiological study designs** such as case-control matching, cohort time-to-event analysis and survey-weighted regression.
- **Spatial epidemiology** helpers for GIS joins, disease mapping and local cluster detection.
- **Infectious disease modelling** via basic SIR simulations for outbreak assessment.
- **Health disparities** indices to quantify risk differences between groups.
- **Syndromic surveillance** utilities to flag anomalous time points in rolling case counts.
- **Environmental health analytics** including simple air pollution exposure modelling.
- **Health economics** helpers for cost-effectiveness calculations.

These functions support population health surveillance and policy evaluation workflows.
